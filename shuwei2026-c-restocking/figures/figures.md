Style: one accent colour per problem (blue, orange, green), Okabe-Ito colours for phases and job families, sans-serif TrueType fonts
(Arial if installed, otherwise Liberation Sans), vector PDF, width 6.6 in. Schematic elements (cards, pictograms, springs, shelves) are
drawn by `code/schem.py`. Every figure was inspected after rendering (overlaps, clipped labels, tick collisions).
Examples are chosen by rules written in the scripts, not by eye: the recorded hand of Fig. 1 and Fig. 3 (`pick_hand_frame`), the record of
Fig. 2 (largest head travel), the window of Fig. 5c (median number of requests, most uneven spacing), the rows of Fig. 6a (every sixth
window after sorting), the trials of Fig. 9 and Fig. 10 (objective closest to the case median).
